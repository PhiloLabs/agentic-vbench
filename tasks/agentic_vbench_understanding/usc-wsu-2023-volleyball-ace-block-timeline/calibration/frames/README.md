# Calibration frames

Sample frames pulled from the baked 720p `match.mp4` at block-point moments, showing
that jersey numbers and the score bug resolve at the shipped resolution (so the task
is doable from the video — the oracle is 1.0 — while still being hard to execute).

Each filename is `set<N>_<USC-WSU score>_block_net.jpg`, captured at the net just
before the scored block:

- `set1_4-2_block_net.jpg`
- `set3_13-17_block_net.jpg` — score bug reads "13 USC | WSU 17", confirming the
  USC-left / WSU-right orientation used for `score_after`
- `set4_10-8_block_net.jpg` — WSU #3 (Basham) and #21 (Radakovic) legible at the net
- `set5_5-11_block_net.jpg`

The score bug never names the blocker or the stuffed hitter — those must be read off
the jerseys in the sub-second net exchange, which is the point of the task.
