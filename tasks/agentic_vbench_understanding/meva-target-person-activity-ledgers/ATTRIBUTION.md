# Attribution and modifications

The source surveillance videos and annotations are from the Multiview Extended
Video with Activities (MEVA) dataset by Kitware Inc. and the Intelligence
Advanced Research Projects Activity (IARPA), released under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0.

- Dataset: https://mevadata.org/
- Data README: https://mevadata.org/resources/README-meva-kf1-data.html
- License: https://mevadata.org/resources/MEVA-data-license.txt

The contributed media is an adaptation made for AgenticVBench. Two public MEVA
clips were divided into one-minute segments, interleaved, concatenated, encoded
without audio, and given a neutral filename. The roster images are crops from
the same source footage. The task does not claim cross-camera global identity.
Segment boundaries are constructed from exact 1,800-frame ranges at 30 fps.
